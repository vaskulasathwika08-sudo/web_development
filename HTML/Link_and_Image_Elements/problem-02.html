Campus Tech Center Unveils Next-Gen AI Innovation Hub

A major leap forward for student-led research and interdisciplinary development

The university has officially opened its state-of-the-art AI Innovation Hub to all enrolled students. This initiative aims to bridge the gap between theoretical computer science and practical, real-world application by providing cutting-edge computing hardware to undergraduate researchers.

Located inside the Central Science & Engineering Building, the facility features advanced high-performance workstations, collaborative meeting pods, and specialized software suites designed for machine learning research.

The grand opening ceremony took place on October 24, 2026, drawing an unprecedented audience of over 500 attending students and faculty members. University leadership confirmed that access will be completely free for all active academic projects starting next week.

This facility was established specifically to address the growing demand for accessible, high-powered computational resources among student researchers. By eliminating financial barriers to high-end hardware, the department hopes to foster groundbreaking innovations directly from the student body.
