Welcome to The Daily Brew Café

About Us

Founded in 2021, The Daily Brew Café is a cozy neighborhood spot dedicated to serving ethically sourced coffee and freshly baked artisanal pastries daily.

Signature Items

  • Organic Ceremonial Matcha Latte - Smooth, Japanese green tea whisked to perfection.

  • Artisanal Double Chocolate Croissant - Golden, flaky layers packed with dark chocolate.

  • Smashed Avocado Toast - Served on toasted sourdough with microgreens and sea salt.

Special Offer

Morning Special: Enjoy a free pastry with any large coffee purchase every 1st Monday of the month!
