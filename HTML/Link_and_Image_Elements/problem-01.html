GLA University

Welcome to GLA University. GLA is a NAACA+ university known for education, research, innovation and a vibrant student community.

About

GLA University is located in greater noida. It provides students with opportunities to learn, conduct research and develop their skills. The university has a diverse community of students and faculty members.

Programs

GLA offers a wide range of undergraduate and graduate programs. Students can study subjects such as Computer Science, Engineering, Business, Medicine, Humanities and Natural Sciences.

Campus Life

Campus life at GLA includes libraries, sports facilities, student clubs, cultural activities and many events. Students can participate in different activities and enjoy a friendly campus environment.
