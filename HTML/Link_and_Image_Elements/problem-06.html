TechFest 2026 - College Technology Festival

Welcome to TechFest 2026 – the biggest technology festival of our college! Join us for an exciting celebration of technology, innovation, creativity and teamwork.

Introduction

TechFest 2026 is a college-level technology festival designed to bring together students who are passionate about technology and innovation. Participants can showcase their skills, learn new technologies and compete in exciting technical events.

Highlights

TechFest 2026 will feature coding competitions, robotics challenges, technical quizzes, project exhibitions, workshops and exciting innovation contests. Students will get an opportunity to work in teams and demonstrate their problem-solving abilities.

Speakers

The festival will feature experienced technology professionals, faculty members and student innovators. Our speakers will share their knowledge about emerging technologies, career opportunities, artificial intelligence, programming and innovation.
