Understanding Personal Accounts & Smart Budgeting

Choosing the correct bank account is the foundation of responsible personal finance. Whether saving for an emergency or managing daily expenses, knowing how interest works will save you money ⟺ over time.

"Compound interest is the eighth wonder of the world. He who understands it, earns it... he who doesn't... pays it." – Anonymous

Key Financial Tips

  • Emergency Fund: Stash away at least 3–6 months of living expenses.
  • Automation: Automatically transfer a percentage of your salary directly to savings.
  • Debt Paydown: Prioritize high-interest card balances to avoid compounding debt.

Step-by-Step Guide to Opening an Account

  1. Research and choose an account structure with zero maintenance fees.
  2. Gather required documentation (ID verification, proof of address).
  3. Submit the digital application or visit a local branch.
  4. Deposit the minimum initial balance to activate the account.

Financial Glossary Rules

Interest Rate
The percentage proportion of a loan charged by a lender to a borrower.
EMI
Equated Monthly Installment; a fixed payment amount made by a borrower to a lender at a specified date each calendar month.
Principal
The original sum of money lent or invested on which interest is calculated.
Tenure
The specified amount of time or duration allocated for full repayment of a loan facility.
