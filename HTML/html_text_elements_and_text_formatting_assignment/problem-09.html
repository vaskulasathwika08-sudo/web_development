Student Terms & Rules

General Rules

Students must carry their official identification card at all times while on campus.
Loss of ID cards must be reported to the administration office immediately.

Respectful behavior toward faculty, staff, and fellow students is required in all university spaces.

Attendance

A minimum of 75% attendance is mandatory for every enrolled course to qualify for final exams.
Falling below 75% attendance will result in automatic course debarment.

Examination

Students must arrive at the examination hall at least 15 minutes prior to the scheduled start time.
Late entry exceeding 30 minutes after commencement is strictly prohibited.

Assignment Submission

All term assignments must be submitted online through the student portal by 11:59 PM on the due date.
Submissions made after the deadline will incur a 10% grade penalty per day late.

Plagiarism and unauthorized collaboration on individual projects are strictly forbidden.
Ensure all external references are properly cited using standard citation guidelines.

Discipline

Unauthorized use of mobile phones or electronic devices during lectures is not permitted.
Devices must be switched off or set to silent mode upon entering classrooms.
