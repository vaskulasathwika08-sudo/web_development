About the college

GLA University provides a supportive learning environment where students can improve their knowledge,creativity and practical skills. It is a well-recognised university which is well known for solid placement records in engineering and management and an "A+" NAAC accreditation.

Courses offered

GLA University offers a wide array of undergraduate,postgraduate,diploma,and doctoral programs across disciplines like Engineering,Management,Pharmacy,Law and Sciences

Campus Life

GLA University is known for maintaining a highly disciplined environment while offering an energetic social and extracurricular culture. Students can participate in cultural programs,technical events,clubs,seminars and other extracurricular activities.
The campus also provides a library,laboratories,classrooms,canteen,and many other facilities for students.
