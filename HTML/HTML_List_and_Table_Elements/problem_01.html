Admission Overview

Our university offers dynamic undergraduate and postgraduate programs designed to foster academic excellence, practical skill development, and innovative research. We welcome applicants from diverse backgrounds who exhibit strong academic potential.

Eligibility

Eligibility
Candidates must have completed their higher secondary education (10+2) or equivalent from a recognized board with a minimum aggregate of 50% marks.
Merit List
Selections are prepared strictly based on academic scores in qualifying exams or recognized national entrance tests.
Counselling
Shortlisted candidates must attend physical or online document verification sessions.
Scholarship
Tuition fee waivers are available for top-ranking merit holders and economically disadvantaged students.
Registration
Final seat allotment requires completing formal university enrollment and fee submission.

Application Process

  1. Visit the official application portal and create a student account.
  2. Fill out the personal and academic background information form.
  3. Upload scanned copies of all required qualification documents.
  4. Pay the non-refundable processing fee online.
  5. Submit the application and download the confirmation slip.

Required Documents

  • High School (10th) Marksheet and Certificate
  • Intermediate (12th) Marksheet or Equivalent Grade Card
  • Transfer or Migration Certificate from previous institution
  • Valid Government-issued Photo ID (Aadhaar Card, Passport, or Voter ID)
  • Recent Passport-size Color Photographs
  • Category or Caste Certificate (if applicable for reservations)
