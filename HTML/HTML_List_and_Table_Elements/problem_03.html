Sequence Before Practical Class

  1. Read the practical instructions.
  2. Understand the experiment.
  3. Prepare the required materials.
  4. Perform the practical work.
  5. Record the observations.
  6. Submit the practical record.

Classroom Rules

  • Students must attend classes regularly.
  • Maintain discipline in the classroom.
  • Bring required books and materials.
  • Submit assignments on time.
  • Follow laboratory safety instructions.

Academic Terms

Lecture
A class where theoretical concepts are explained.
Lab
A practical session where students perform experiments.
Tutorial
A session for practice and solving problems.
Internal Assessment
An assessment conducted during the semester.
Practical
A session focused on practical application of concepts.
