Science Notes

Chemistry

Chemistry is the study of matter and its interactions. Common chemical formulas include:

  • Water: H2O
  • Carbon Dioxide: CO2
  • Oxygen Gas: O2
  • Sulfuric Acid: H2SO4
  • Methane: CH4

During a chemical reaction, reactants transform into products.

Physics

Physics deals with energy, force, and spatial relationships.

Area is measured in m2, while volume is calculated in m3. Einstein's famous mass-energy equivalence equation is E = mc2.

The acceleration due to gravity is an important physical constant.

Biology

Biology is the study of living organisms and life processes.

Photosynthesis is the process used by plants to convert light energy into chemical energy.

Plants absorb water (H2O) and carbon dioxide (CO2) to release oxygen (O2). The primary cell organelle responsible for energy generation is the mitochondria.

Quick Revision

Key Takeaways: Always balance chemical reactions, use SI units like m2 and m3 for measurement, and remember that DNA stores genetic information in living organisms.
